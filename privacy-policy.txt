# UnionMC Bot Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 26, 2026

## 1. Introduction

UnionMC Bot ("the Bot") is a Discord bot associated with the UnionMC Minecraft community. It provides server-status monitoring, utility commands, moderation, and security-related functionality.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Bot may process, how it is used, and how privacy-related requests may be submitted.

## 2. Information Processed

Depending on its features, the Bot may process:

- Minecraft usernames and player presence information, including whether a player is online.
- Minecraft server status and player-count information.
- Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, and display names.
- Command inputs and interactions, including commands such as `/playercount`, `/ping`, and `/help`.
- Messages or text that are processed by moderation systems.
- Moderation events, actions, and relevant information needed to enforce server rules.
- Technical information necessary for operation, troubleshooting, and security.

## 3. Server Monitoring

The Bot may monitor the UnionMC Minecraft server to determine its availability and detect players currently online.

This information may be used to:

- Display the current player count.
- Report player presence.
- Respond to server-status commands.
- Provide server-related information to Discord users.

Player detection is used for server functionality and is not intended to collect unrelated personal information.

## 4. Commands and Functionality

The Bot may provide commands including, but not limited to:

- `/playercount` — Reports Minecraft server player information.
- `/ping` — Checks Bot responsiveness.
- `/help` — Displays available commands and information.

Command inputs and interaction data may be processed to provide the requested functionality.

## 5. Moderation and Security

The Bot may process messages or relevant activity to detect violations of UnionMC's rules, including:

- Direct profanity where prohibited.
- Abusive, harassing, or inappropriate conduct.
- Manipulation or deceptive behavior.
- Attempts to redirect users to other chat platforms in violation of server rules.
- Other prohibited behavior or security-related violations.

When violations are detected, the Bot may take automated moderation actions, such as removing content, issuing warnings, restricting access, or other actions supported by its configuration.

Moderation-related information may be processed to protect the community, enforce rules, and investigate abuse or security incidents.

## 6. Storage and Retention

Information may be processed temporarily or stored where necessary for the Bot's operation, moderation, security, and troubleshooting.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and its purpose.

**Discord user IDs, Usernames, Minecraft Usernames, Minecraft UUIDs are all stored for the following: Playercount Visibility, Ping, Discord-to-Minecraft chatting, vice versa. It is retained solely for the purpose
monitoring. It is also retained for as long as the player is online [Minecraft UUID and Username.], Discord user ID is stored up until they activate Discord-to-Minecraft chat mode.**

## 7. Sharing of Information

Information is not intentionally sold.

Information may be processed by services required to operate the Bot, including Discord, Minecraft server infrastructure, and hosting or service providers.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to address security incidents, abuse, or legal obligations.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Privacy Requests

Users may contact the Bot's operator regarding privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with them.

Requests for deletion may be considered where applicable, subject to legitimate moderation, security, and operational requirements.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not designed to request sensitive personal information from users.

Users should not submit passwords, payment information, government identification, or other sensitive information through the Bot.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Bot's functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published at the policy's designated location.
